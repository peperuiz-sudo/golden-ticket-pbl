The Golden Ticket: Picking the Ultimate Destination

THE GOLDEN TICKET

Project Overview & Mission

Welcome to the Golden Ticket Challenge!
In this project, you will become a travel agent for a day. Your mission? To defend an international destination (London, Dublin, Edinburgh, etc.) and convince your classmates to spend their "Golden Ticket" there.

You will use English to compare landmarks, find the best prices, and prove that your city is the most exciting or the easiest to visit.

Level: A2 (EOI)

Duration: 2 Sessions

Final Product: A digital travel comparison and a live "pitch" to the class.
