TEACHER'S GUIDE (Guía didáctica)

Pedagogical Planning (2 Sessions)

Roadmap: Our 2-Session Challenge
Session 1: Research & Design (135 min)

Phase 1 (45 min): Group formation and city discovery using the "Digital Explorer Platform".
Phase 2 (90 min): Creating the Genially presentation and the Comparison Chart. Focus on using comparatives and superlatives.
Session 2: Pitching & Voting (135 min)

Phase 1 (45 min): Final touches and rehearsal. Peer-feedback (check each other's drafts).
Phase 2 (75 min): The Travel Fair! 3-minute oral pitches by each group.
Phase 3 (15 min): The "Golden Ticket" voting ceremony and winner announcement.

Curricular Integration (EOI A2)

To ensure the students meet the A2 Level requirements (EOI), this project assesses the following language activities:

Written Comprehension (Reading): Ability to identify relevant information in simple travel descriptions, landmarks, and pricing tables.
Oral Production (The Pitch): Ability to produce short, prepared presentations about a travel destination using basic connectors and descriptive vocabulary.
Written Production (The Comparison Chart): Ability to write simple, descriptive texts and comparisons using comparative and superlative adjectives.
Socio-cultural Knowledge: Identifying landmarks and practical travel information in English-speaking countries.
